Vice Cover Studio: turn a photo and a caption into multi-panel cover art in the style of a Vice City sunset poster, exported as a PNG. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6 Cover Art" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## What it does

Vice Cover Studio is a web app that turns your own photos into poster-style cover art for the fictional city of Palma Bay on the Neon Coast. Load up to 3 photos from your machine (they never leave it), or try the two bundled samples. Pick one of 3 layouts (Cover, Grid, Solo), one of 4 colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Golden Hour, Noir), crop and position each panel with a zoom slider and drag-to-pan, edit the title and tagline, and export a PNG in 1080x1080, 1080x1350 or 1920x1080, one at a time or all three at once.

The title is set in Anton (SIL Open Font License, bundled under app/fonts/), with a sunset gradient fill, dark outline and drop shadow. Everything runs offline from static files under app/, with no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.
